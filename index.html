Our Process & Craftsmanship

A measured path from first conversation to finished installation — designed for clarity, not spectacle.

  1. 01

    Consultation & 3D Design

    Site review, spatial planning, and photoreal concepts that lock proportions before a single board is cut.

  2. 02

    Material Selection

    Veneers, solids, and lacquer samples studied under your light — grain direction matched to the architecture.

  3. 03

    Precision Workshop Fabrication

    CNC accuracy paired with hand-fit joinery. Every panel, edge, and reveal is checked against the drawing set.

  4. 04

    On-site Professional Installation

    Protected delivery, level lines, and silent hardware tuning until doors close with the weight they should.
